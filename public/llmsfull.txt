## Important facts

- Hawiyat is based in Algeria and supports customers in Arabic, French, and English. These are support languages, not translated site content.
- Billing is in Algerian dinars (DZD); local payment methods CCP and Baridi Mob are accepted, as well as USD.
- Models (GPT, Claude, Gemini, open models) are routes on the execution layer, never sold as SKUs or credits. Hawiyat does not resell or rebrand any model.
- Hawiyat does not imply an official partnership with any model provider unless explicitly stated; third-party product and model names belong to their respective owners.
- Orders are placed through the Hawiyat website; the team activates Composer workspaces and deployments within 24 hours.
